Summary of Key Points:
Purpose of the Document

The circular defines the calculation methods for duties, taxes, and fees collected by the Customs Administration at importation.
It updates Circular No. 10/DGD/CAB/D.420 dated 01/06/1994 due to changes in fiscal levies.


Types of Levies


Customs Duties (DD):

Applied to imported/exported goods based on their customs value (ad-valorem).
Rates: 0%, 5%, 15%, and 30%.
Formula: DD = (Customs Value × Rate) / 100.



Taxes on Turnover (TCA):

Includes VAT (17% normal rate, 7% reduced rate), Internal Consumption Tax (TIC), Tax on Petroleum Products (TPP), etc.
VAT is calculated on the customs value plus all other taxes, excluding VAT itself.



Other Fiscal and Parafiscal Taxes:

Examples: Tax on tobacco products (TAPT), cereals and legumes (TCLS), fuel, tires, oils, and plastic bags.
These are specific taxes based on quantity, weight, or volume.



Indirect Taxes:

Examples: Tax on meat (TSV), circulation tax on alcohol and wine (DCA), tax on batteries (DPE), and guarantee rights on precious metals.



Fees (RPS):

Fees for customs services, infrastructure use, and residence changes.




Key Tax Rates

Customs Duties: 0%, 5%, 15%, 30%.
VAT: 17% (normal), 7% (reduced).
TIC: 10%-50% (ad-valorem) or specific rates (e.g., 1260 DA/kg for cigarettes).
TPP: 1 DA/HL.
TCLS: 15 DA/quintal.
TAPT: 6 DA/pack.
Carburants Tax: 0.10 DA/L (leaded gasoline), 0.30 DA/L (diesel).
Tires Tax: 5 DA/light vehicle tire, 10 DA/heavy vehicle tire.
Oils Tax: 12,500 DA/ton.
Plastic Bags Tax: 10.50 DA/kg.
Meat Tax (TSV): 5 DA/kg.
Alcohol Circulation Tax (DCA): 1000 DA/HL for alcohol, 4000 DA/HL for wine.
Battery Tax (DPE): 20% of total value (including all taxes).
Guarantee Rights: 20,000 DA/100g (platinum), 8,000 DA/100g (gold), 300 DA/100g (silver).


Exclusions from VAT

Certain taxes (e.g., TAPT, DPE, TSV, TSP, T.PNEUS, T.HUILES) are excluded from the VAT base.


RPS Fees

200 DA for import declarations.
100 DA for export declarations.
500 DA for summary declarations (manifests).
SIGAD usage fee: 5 DA/minute.
Annual subscription fee: 20,000 DA.


Abrogated Taxes

Precompte tax (2% in 1997, increased to 4% in 2003) was abolished by Article 40 of the 2008 Finance Law.


General Notes

All duties and taxes are calculated based on the exchange rate at the time of declaration.
The document includes detailed examples for calculating various taxes and duties.

This circular serves as a comprehensive guide for understanding the legal framework and calculation methods for import duties and taxes in Algeria.
