6.2 TERMINAL HANDLING AT THE CONTAINER TERMINAL
The following services are covered:
- Acceptance/delivery of the container at the terminal
- Stacking/Destacking, making reefer connections
- Conveyance between the stack and ship
- Base Tariff
- Port security
