ARCHIVE — Afrique du Sud : valeur retenue pour la TVA à l'importation (relevé du 02/10/2026)

1. Value-Added Tax Act 89 of 1991, section 13(2) — texte consolidé au 01/08/2021
   URL : https://disasterlaw.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/media/disaster_law/2021-10/89%20of%201991%20VALUE-ADDED%20TAX%20ACT_2021.08.01%20-%20to%20date.pdf
   sha256 du PDF (250 p., non versé au dépôt) : 7b9012372368d1ae5fcf7df581733124957a44a441f3d4f0a2cb2b3d7eb08093
   VERBATIM (p. 93-94) :
   « (2) For the purposes of this Act the value to be placed on the importation of goods into the
   Republic shall be deemed to be –
   (a) where such goods are entered or are required to be entered for home consumption in terms of
   the Customs and Excise Act, the value thereof for customs duty purposes, plus any duty levied
   in terms of the said Act in respect of the importation of such goods, plus 10 per cent of the said
   value; or
   (b) where such goods have their origin in Botswana, Lesotho, Swaziland or Namibia and are imported
   from such a country, the amount of the value as contemplated in paragraph (a), except that such
   value shall not be increased by the factor of 10 per cent: »
   Réserve du même article : « Provided that where the Minister has made a regulation determining the
   value of such goods for the purposes of this section, the greater of such determined value or the
   value declared on importation shall be used instead of the value for customs purposes. »

2. SARS, guide « Value-Added Tax Levied on the Importation of Goods into South Africa » (CE-G06)
   URL : https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/Legal-CE-G06-Value-added-tax-levied-on-the-importation-of-goods-into-South-Africa.pdf
   Fichier : ZAF_sars_guide_TVA_importation_CE-G06.pdf
   sha256 : 0a6552b6ee4570fe41eed781c950f4606c06caebc56dd48d56567ae67fdabff2
   VERBATIM (§ 5.1) : « [(Customs Value + 10% thereof) + (any non-rebated duties levied on the goods)]
   = ATV. ATV × 15% = VAT payable. » ; « The 10% upliftment is not an amount payable to SARS, but
   represents an amount in lieu of transport and insurance costs which is used for calculating the ATV. »
   ; exemple 2 (origine BELN) : « Customs value R1 000 — 10% of customs value Not applicable — Duty Not
   applicable – SACU — Added tax value (ATV) R1 000 ».
   NB : le guide indique lui-même « This guide has no binding legal effect » ; il corrobore la loi.

LECTURE : la valeur en douane SACU est la valeur FOB (fiche SACU_assiette_DD_2026-09-17.json).
Assiette TVA = FOB × 1,10 + droits ; origine Botswana, Lesotho, Eswatini ou Namibie importée de ces
pays = FOB + droits, sans majoration.
