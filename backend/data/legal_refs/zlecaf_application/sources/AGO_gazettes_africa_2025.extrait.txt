###### [Gazettes.Africa](https://gazettes.africa/)

Gazettes.Africa is a project of [Laws.Africa opens in new tab](https://laws.africa/) and [AfricanLII opens in new tab](https://africanlii.org/).

🔥 A fire destroyed hundreds of thousands of African gazettes. We're asking librarians and funders to help preserve Gazettes before more are lost.

[How you can help](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XPTFNkQh5ytpTQoutx4GEU5wNV4fqWbl/view)

We welcome donations of gazette collections. We will scan and digitise gazettes for free. Please [get in touch](mailto:info@laws.africa) to donate gazettes.
