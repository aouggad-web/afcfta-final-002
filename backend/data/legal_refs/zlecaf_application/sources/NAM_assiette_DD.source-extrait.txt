s. 75(1) — assiette : valeur transactionnelle
---------------------------------------------
the value for customs duty purposes of any imported goods shall, within the meaning of section 76 and at the time of entry for home consumption, be the transaction value of such goods

s. 77(1)(e) — AJOUT jusqu'au port d'exportation
-----------------------------------------------
the cost of transportation, loading, unloading, handling and insurance, and associated costs incidental to delivery of the goods at the port or place of export in the country of export, and placing such goods on board a ship or on any vehicle, or in a container as defined in section 1(2), at such port or place

s. 77(2)(a) — DÉDUCTION du fret et de l'assurance internationaux
----------------------------------------------------------------
the cost of transportation and the cost of loading, unloading, handling and insurance, and associated costs incidental to the transportation of the goods from Republic of Namibia 88 Annotated Statutes Customs and Excise Act 20 of 1998 the port or place of export in the country of export to the port or place of import in Namibia

s. 131(1) + Schedule 9 — abrogation du texte sud-africain reçu
--------------------------------------------------------------
the laws specified in Schedule 9 are hereby repealed to the extent set out in the third column of that Schedule
