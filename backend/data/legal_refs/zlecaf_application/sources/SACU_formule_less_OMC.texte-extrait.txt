1. WTO Secretariat, Trade Policy Review — Southern African Customs Union, WT/TPR/S/324, 30 September 2015
(https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s324_e.pdf, sha256 64f71316c10230690d06e88d7f21ca13cd79da974edffca661cb21b9f5b1d3ff ; non archivé, 0,3 Mo) :

3.19. As at the time of the previous TPR of SACU, variable (formula) duties are levied on
corn-flour, preserved tomatoes, cherries, and two kinds of tobacco (Table 3.3). They pursue the
objective of maintaining domestic prices above a certain level and protecting local industry against
low international prices. They are based on a relationship between the f.o.b. import prices and
reference prices. In other words: they are inversely proportional to import prices.3
