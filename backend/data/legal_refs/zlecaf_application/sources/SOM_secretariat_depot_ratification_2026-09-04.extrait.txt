## Somalia Becomes 50th State Party to the AfCFTA Agreement

_4 September 2026 | Accra, Ghana_— A significant milestone has been reached in Africa’s economic integration journey as the Federal Republic of Somalia deposited its Instrument of Ratification of the Agreement Establishing the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) on 4 September 2026, becoming the 50th State Party to the AfCFTA Agreement.

Somalia’s ratification strengthens the collective commitment of African countries to establishing one integrated continental market and expanding opportunities for trade, investment and shared prosperity across the continent.

The AfCFTA Secretariat welcomes Somalia as the 50th State Party and looks forward to working with the Government of Somalia and relevant stakeholders to advance implementation and support Somali businesses in accessing opportunities within the continental market.

Somalia’s accession further broadens the reach of the AfCFTA and reinforces the ambition of creating a single African market that connects businesses, consumers and economies across the continent.

54 Signatories. 50 State Parties. One Continental Market.
