Art. 2 : There shall be levied, in accordance with the provisions of this Regulation, a social welfare levy on all goods imported into Ethiopia specified in the Customs Tariffs except those exempted under Article 6 of this Regulation.
Art. 4 : 1/ Subject to Article 6 of this Regulation, Social Welfare Levy of 3 percent shall be levied and collected on goods imported in accordance with this Regulation. 2/ The Social Welfare Levy levied on imported goods under this Regulation is in addition to any other customs duties and taxes collected under applicable laws.
Art. 5 : 1/ The basis of computation for the Social Welfare Levy levied under this Regulation shall be the aggregate of cost, insurance and freight (CIF) value. 2/ The Social Welfare Levy shall not serve as a base for any other tax imposed on imported goods.
Art. 6 : The following shall be exempted from the Social Welfare Levy: 1/ persons and organizations with diplomatic privileges; 2/ goods that are subject to the sur tax levied under Import Sur-Tax Council of Ministers Regulation No. 133/2007; and 3/ goods exempt by a Directive to be issued by the Ministry of Finance due to social and economic reasons.
Art. 8 : This Regulation shall enter into force as of the 6th Day of August, 2022.

Surtax on imports
Surtax is governed by Council of Ministers Regulation No. 133/2007. The tax is levied on imported goods at a flat rate of 10% (of the sum of the CIF value of imports, duty payable, excise payable and VAT payable). However, certain imports are exempt from surtax payments. These include:
▪ fertilisers;
▪ petroleum and lubricants;
▪ motor vehicles for freight and passengers (including buses), and special-purpose motor vehicles;
▪ aircraft, spacecraft, and parts thereof;
▪ capital and investment goods.
The Ministry of Finance has also excluded additional basic goods such as groundnuts, potatoes, sugar, meat and certain types of machinery from surtax payment in subsequent amendments.
[...]
▪ Surtax-free privileges for international organisations and their foreign personnel, under the Vienna Conventions, are part of the benchmark system. Imports by diplomats, consular officers, and their immediate family members are exempt from all surtax.
