PUBLIC NOTICE
UPDATE ON THE VAT CHANGES
Members of the public are informed that the Minister of Finance, Honorable Peggy Serame has issued an extension on the 
decrease in the VAT rate through Statutory Instrument No. 5 of 2023 published on the 13th January 2023.
In terms of the said Order, the reduced rate of VAT payable (12 per cent) in terms of Section 7(1) of the VAT Act shall be 
in place from the 3rd February 2023 until the 31st March 2023.
No similar extension has been made for cooking oil and Liquid Petroleum Gas (LPG) which were zero-rated through VAT 
(Amendment) Act No. 23 of 2022. The two (2) commodities will therefore revert to the standard rate of 12 per cent with 
effect from the 3rd February 2023.
For more information, contact the BURS Call Centre at 17649
BURS, collecting for progress
