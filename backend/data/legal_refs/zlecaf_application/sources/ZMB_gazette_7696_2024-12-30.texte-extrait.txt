GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA 
Statutory INSTRUMENT No. 85 or 2024 
The Customs and Excise Act 
(Laws, Volume 18, Cap. 322) 
The Customs and Excise (Precious Metals) (Export Duty) 
(Suspension) (Revocation) Order, 2024 
IN EXERCISE of the powers contained in section 89 of the Customs 
and Excise Act, the following Order is made: 
1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Customs and Excise 
(Precious Metals) (Export Duty) (Suspension) (Revocation) Order, 
2024. 
(2) This Order shall come into operation on Ist January, 2025. 
2. The Customs and Excise (Precious Metals) (Export Duty) 
(Suspension) Order, 2020, is revoked.
