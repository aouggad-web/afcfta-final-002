=== Special Import Levy (Amendment) Act, 2025 (Act 1125) ===
Source : https://lite.judy.legal/legislation/akn/gh/act/2025/1125 — publié au Government Gazette le 2 avril 2025, en vigueur le 2 avril 2025.
AN ACT to amend the Special Import Levy Act, 2013 (Act 861) to extend the period of application of the Special Import Levy to the end of 2028.
1. Section 1 of Act 861 amended
The Special Import Levy Act, 2013 (Act 861) is amended by the substitution for section 1, of
"Imposition of Special Import Levy
1. There is imposed by this Act for the years 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026, 2027 and 2028, a Special Import Levy on imported goods to be paid at the point of entry and to be computed on the Cost, Insurance and Freight value of the goods."
