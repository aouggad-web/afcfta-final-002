PUBLIC NOTICE
REVERTING TO 14 PER CENT VAT RATE 
VAT Registered persons and the public are informed that the reprieve measure to reduce the VAT rate from 14 per cent to 
12 per cent that was introduced in the month of August 2022 is coming to an end on 31 March 2023.
Therefore, with effect from 01 April 2023, all VAT registered persons will now be required by law to charge VAT at the 
rate of 14 per cent.
BURS will issue transitional guidelines to guide taxpayers on how to transition from the 12 per cent rate to 14 per cent 
rate. The guidelines will be made available through the BURS website www.burs.org.bw
For more information, contact the BURS Call Centre at 17649.
BURS, collecting for progress
