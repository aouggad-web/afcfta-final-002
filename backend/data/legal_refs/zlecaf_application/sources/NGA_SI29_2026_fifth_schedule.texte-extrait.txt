5. The Sixth Schedule to the Principal Act is substituted by a new Schedule as Export Prohibition List.
6. The Customs, Excise Tariff, Etc. (Variation) Order 2023 is hereby revoked.
7.—(i) The First, Third and Sixth Schedules of the Principal Act varied by this Order shall come into force on the 1st of May, 2026.
(ii) The Fifth Schedule of the Principal Act varied by this Order shall come into force on the 1st July, 2026; while the rates for 2027 and 2028 shall take effect on 1st January of each year.
