--- Act 91 of 1964, chapitre IX « Value » ---

65. Value for customs duty purposes
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the value for duty purposes of any imported goods
shall be the domestic value or the free on board price thereof, whichever is the greater.

67. Free on board price
Subject to the provisions of this Act, the free on board price of any imported goods shall be the
price charged in respect of such goods by the exporter plus all the costs and charges incidental to
the sale in question and to placing such goods on board ship or on any vehicle ready for
exportation and any agent's commission (calculated on such price, costs and charges) in respect of
such goods.

--- SARS SC-CR-A-03 (rév. 5, 2025-12-11) ---

« The primary basis for the Customs value under the Agreement is transaction value, which is the
price actually paid or payable. »

« The transaction value [Section 66(1)] definition clearly states that the transaction value of
imported goods shall be the price actually paid or payable for the imported goods. This is a real
price which is actually made and not a normal price for imported goods. »

--- Point d'attention (FOB vs CIF) ---

La base retenue pour le droit de douane SACU est la valeur de transaction sur base FOB : le coût
du transport et de l'assurance internationaux (du lieu d'exportation au lieu d'importation) est
exclu. Ce n'est pas la valeur CIF. Le choix du membre (Article 8.2 de l'Accord OMC sur la valeur
en douane) adopté par l'Afrique du Sud/SACU est la base FOB. La règle précise de la méthode 1
(fret/assurance exclus) est détaillée dans la politique SC-CR-A-05, non encore archivée ; le
texte historique de l'article 65(1) (« domestic value or free on board price ») et la déduction
ci-dessus l'établissent.
