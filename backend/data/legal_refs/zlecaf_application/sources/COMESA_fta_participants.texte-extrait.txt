COMESA launched a Free Trade Area on 31 October 2000 when nine Member States
elimination their tariffs on goods originating in COMESA, in accordance with
the tariff liberalisation schedule adopted in 1992.

Member states
As of 2026, 18 COMESA member states participating in the Free Trade Area
(FTA): Burundi, Comoros, Djibouti, Egypt, Eritrea, Eswatini, Kenya, Libya,
Madagascar, Malawi, Mauritius, Rwanda, Seychelles, Somalia, Sudan, Tunisia,
Uganda, Zambia and Zimbabwe
