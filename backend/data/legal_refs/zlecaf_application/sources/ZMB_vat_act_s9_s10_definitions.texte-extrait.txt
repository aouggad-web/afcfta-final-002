9.  (1)  Tax on a taxable supply of goods or services shall be charged on their
taxable value, at the prescribed rate of tax.
Rate of tax
(2)  Tax on a taxable importation of goods shall be charged on their taxable value, at
the prescribed rate of tax.
(3)  The prescribed rate of tax shall be seventeen and a half per centum, unless the
Minister, by statutory order, determines a lower rate.
(4)  For the purposes of any provision of this Act, or of the regulations or rules made
under this Act, the prescribed rate of tax in the case of a zero-rated supply shall be
regarded as zero.
