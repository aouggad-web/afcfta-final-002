[…] milestone has been reached in Africa’s economic integration journey as the Federal Republic of Somalia deposited its Instrument of Ratification of the Agreement Establishing the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) on 4 September 2026, becoming the 50th State Party to the AfCFTA Agreement. Somalia’s ratification strengthens the collective commitment of African countries to establishing one integrated continental market and expanding opportunities for trade, investment and shared prosperity across the continent. The AfCFTA Secretariat welcomes Somalia as the 50th State Party and looks forward to working with the Government of Somalia and relevant stakeholders to advance implementation and support Somali businesses in accessing opportunities within the continental market. […] Mogadishu, (SONNA) — The Federal Republic of Somalia has officially deposited its instrument of ratification of the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) Agreement, becoming the 50th country to formally ratify the landmark continental trade pact. The Minister of Trade and Industry of the Federal Government of Somalia, H.E. Amb. Gamal Mohamed Hassan, formally submitted the ratification instrument to the African Union during a ceremony held at the AU headquarters in Addis Ababa. The submission completes Somalia’s formal ratification process following approval of the AfCFTA Agreement by the Federal Parliament and its subsequent signing by the President of the Federal Republic of Somalia, H.E.
