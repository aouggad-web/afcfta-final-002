BURS — Zero-Rated Foodstuff under VAT Act of 2026 (Botswana, avis du 9 juillet 2026)
Extrait du texte intégral du PDF (source : burs.org.bw, archivé en face) :
---
The public is informed of the revised list of foodstuffs that are subject to VAT at a zero rate 
under the Value Added Tax (VAT) Act, 2026 which commenced on the 1st July 2026. 
• A supply of the following in their natural state and not mixed with other products are  
  subject to VAT at a zero rate:
  • Sorghum or maize meal (except when sold as prepared or cooked food)
  • Millet grain
  • Millet meal
  • Wheat grain
  • Maize cobs
  • Sugar
  • Brown bread
  • Fresh vegetables: potatoes, tomatoes, onions, cabbage, rape, beetroots, carrots, and 
    spinach (excluding dried, canned or bottled vegetables)
  • Fresh fruits: oranges, apples and bananas (excluding dried, canned or bottled fruit and 
    nuts)
  • Rice
  • Samp 
  • Bread flour (white, brown and whole wheat)
  • Cooking oil
NB: Any food items not appearing in the above list are subject to VAT at 14%. 
Retailers and other affected businesses are required to take note of the above changes 
and make the necessary adjustments to their systems and pricing arrangements.
