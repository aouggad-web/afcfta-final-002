EAST AFRICAN COMMUNITY GAZETTE
THE EAST AFRICAN COMMUNITY CUSTOMS MANAGEMENT ACT 2004
LEGAL NOTICE
IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred upon the Council of Ministers by Section 114 (3) of the East African Community Customs 
Management Act, 2004, the Council of Ministers has amended the Exemptions Regime under the Fifth Schedule in;
(a) PART A – SPECIFIC EXEMPTIONS, in paragraph 2, to insert immediately after the word Police, “a comma (,)”  to be followed 
immediately thereafter by the words “National Intelligence Services Agencies” ; and
(b) PART B – GENERAL EXEMPTIONS, in;
(i) paragraph 1(a), to insert immediately after the words “paper napkins,”  the words “and paper specially designed and 
exclusively used for printing passenger boarding passes” ; and
(ii) “paragraph 20, to replace the words Director / Commissioner  with the words “Competent Authority” .
RT. HON. REBECCA ALITWALA KADAGA 
Chairperson Council of Ministers

LEGAL NOTICE
In EXERCISE of the powers conferred upon the Council of Ministers by Section 140 of the East African Community Customs Man -
agement Act 2004, the Council of Ministers issues this notice.
Stay of the application of the conditions specified in item 8 of Legal Notice No. EAC/39/2013
The application of the conditions specified in Item 8 of Legal Notice No. EAC/39/2013 published on 30th June, 2013 relating to the 
remission of duty on Completely Knocked Down (CKD) kits for motorcycle assembly is hereby stayed for a period of one year.
