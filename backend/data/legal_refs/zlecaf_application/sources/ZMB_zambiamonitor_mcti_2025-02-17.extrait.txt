1) « Zambia has officially gazetted its Provisional Schedule of Tariff Concessions under the
African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) Agreement, paving the way for trade under the
continental pact. The concessions, enacted through Statutory Instrument (SI) No. 92 of
December 30, 2024, mark Zambia's readiness to participate in AfCFTA, following the earlier
introduction of SI No. 94. Ministry of Commerce, Trade, and Industry Principal Public
Relations Officer, Everness Nankala, confirmed the development in a statement issued in
Lusaka on Monday, saying the move expands Zambia's preferential market access beyond
COMESA and SADC. »
