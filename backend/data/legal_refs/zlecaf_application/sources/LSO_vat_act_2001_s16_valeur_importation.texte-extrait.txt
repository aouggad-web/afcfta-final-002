VERBATIM (s.16 « Taxable Value of a Taxable Import ») :
« (1) Subject to subsection (4), the taxable value of a taxable import of goods is the sum of -
(a) the value of the goods ascertained for the purposes of customs or excise duty under the Customs
and Excise Act, 1982 whether or not any duty is payable on those goods;
(b) the amount of customs or excise duty, or any other fiscal charge (other than value added tax
payable under this Act), if any, payable on those goods; and
(c) the value of any services to which section 8(9) applies which is not otherwise included in the
taxable value under paragraph (a) »

LECTURE : assiette TVA = valeur en douane (FOB, SACU) + droits et taxes, la TVA exclue.
