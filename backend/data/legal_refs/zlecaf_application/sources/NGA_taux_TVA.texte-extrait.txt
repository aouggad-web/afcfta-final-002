Eleventh Schedule, Sections 186 (2) — ITEMS ON WHICH TAX IS SUSPENDED UNDER THIS ACT
1. Charging and collection of VAT on the following items shall commence on the date indicated by the Minister, or suspended where it is expedient to do so in the public interest, by an order issued in the Official Gazette – (a) petroleum products; (b) renewable energy equipment; (c) compressed natural gas (CNG); (d) Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG); and (e) other gaseous hydrocarbons.
